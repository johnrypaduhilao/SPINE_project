POLICY MODEL
Every policy is a tuple P = (D, E, A, C(R, S, T), CT):

  D  = Definer   : the entity that generates the policy and is accountable for it,
                   and that a trace can resolve to a real part of the system. The
                   declarative root (P0) is defined by the human operator (or
                   user) who issues the intent. Every refined policy (definitive
                   or imperative) is defined by the assurance engine that
                   generates it. Do NOT name external parties (regulators,
                   standards bodies, data owners): accountability must point
                   inside the system. The definer differs by level - human
                   operator at the root, assurance engine below.

  E  = Enforcer  : the single component that carries the policy out. The
                   declarative root is enforced by the Orchestrator; definitive
                   policies by Monitor, Analyze, or Plan; imperative policies by
                   Execute.

  A  = Action    : the verb only - one operation (e.g., classify, tag, monitor,
                   block, decelerate). The object the action applies to is NOT
                   part of the action; it goes in the Resource. The location goes
                   in Spatial and the time in Temporal. An imperative is fired by
                   a monitored runtime condition (e.g., an unauthorized request, a
                   threshold crossing, detected fog); that condition is observed
                   state, recorded by observability at run time, and is NOT part
                   of the policy - only the verb is.

  C  = Constraints, of three kinds (each may hold multiple values):
        R = Resource : the object the action operates on - a concrete entity such
                       as a file, an operation, an actor, a vehicle, or a CPU. A
                       resource may carry attributes (a file's name, path,
                       permission, sensitivity); model them but you need not list
                       them. A deadline or time window is NOT a resource - it is
                       Temporal.
        S = Spatial  : location only (path, lane, geofence, address, region,
                       host).
        T = Temporal : time only. Use "always" when the policy is in force with no
                       time restriction (the default); a deadline; or a time
                       window (e.g., 03:00-08:00). Cadence words - "continuous",
                       "per operation", "on match", "per cycle" - are NOT
                       temporal: they are either "always" or a firing condition.

  CT = Context   : the operating mode that selects which policy applies (e.g.,
                   production vs. staging, normal vs. emergency, privilege level).
                   Runtime situational factors - current weather, current traffic,
                   a value crossing a threshold - are NOT context; they are the
                   monitored state that fires a rule (see Action), not a policy
                   field.

State is NOT a tuple field. The monitored runtime condition (which actor, which
file, the current weather, a threshold crossing) is observability data captured
at run time, not part of any policy.

ABSTRACTION LEVELS (a policy is exactly one of these)
  declarative : states the goal/what, no mechanism (the root); enforced by the
                Orchestrator.
  definitive  : specifies what to monitor / analyze / decide, still
                mechanism-agnostic; enforced by Monitor, Analyze, or Plan.
  imperative  : a concrete executable rule; enforced by Execute.

REFINEMENT RULES
  - The root is the intent restated as a single declarative policy (P0), defined
    by the human operator.
  - Each policy may have children that refine it toward execution; every refined
    policy is defined by the assurance engine.
  - Every non-root policy names its parent_id (this forms the chain of causality).
  - Every policy includes a one-sentence rationale: WHY it follows from its
    parent.
  - Constraint values are not fixed down a branch. As a policy is refined, its
    resource, spatial, and temporal values may narrow, widen, or change to fit the
    child's specific job - a parent may scope "all lanes" while a child scopes one
    lane, or a parent hold "always" while a child acts only in a window. Carry a
    value down unchanged only when the child genuinely shares it; do not copy a
    parent's R, S, or T into a child by default.
